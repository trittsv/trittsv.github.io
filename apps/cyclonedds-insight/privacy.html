Privacy Policy

CycloneDDS Insight App

Last updated: Sun 6. Sep 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the CycloneDDS Insight Android app handles information. The app is provided by Sven Trittler. For privacy questions, contact trittsv.apps@gmx.de.

CycloneDDS Insight is a technical tool for discovering, inspecting, and interacting with systems using the Data Distribution Service (DDS) protocol.

2. Information processed by the app

To provide its functionality, the app processes information from the DDS networks it joins. Depending on the connected systems and the features you use, this may include:

  • DDS domain, participant, reader, and writer identifiers; topic names; data types; and quality-of-service settings.
  • Network addresses, host or application information advertised by participants, and communication statistics.
  • Message contents received from subscribed topics, and message contents you enter or generate for publication.
  • App preferences, configuration files, and files you choose to open or export.
  • Local diagnostic information, such as timestamps, software versions, errors, and details of app operations.

DDS messages may contain personal or sensitive information, depending on what the connected systems publish.

3. Network communication and recipients

The app sends and receives DDS discovery traffic when joining configured domains, including domains restored at startup. This allows other reachable DDS participants to discover the app. When you subscribe to or publish a topic, data is exchanged with matching participants.

Statistics features may also make HTTP requests to diagnostic endpoints associated with DDS participants. Communication is not necessarily restricted to the local network: its reach depends on your network and DDS configuration.

The app does not automatically upload DDS messages or local diagnostic logs to a developer-operated backend. Other participants and diagnostic endpoint operators receive information as part of the network functionality described above. Their handling and retention of that information are outside the app developer's control.

4. Local storage and deletion

App preferences and diagnostic logs are stored locally on your device. Logs may contain information about your DDS environment. Log files are rotated at approximately 5 MB, with a retention setting of seven rotated files.

You can remove app-managed local data through Android's Settings by clearing the app's storage. Uninstalling the app normally removes its app-private data. Files exported to other locations must be deleted separately. Android backup and restore behavior depends on your device and system settings.

Deleting local data does not delete messages, logs, or copies already retained by other network participants or people with whom you shared files.

5. Permissions

The app uses network and Wi-Fi-related permissions to communicate with DDS systems, inspect connectivity, and receive multicast traffic. These permissions support discovery and message exchange.

6. Advertising, analytics, and accounts

The Android app does not require a user account and does not include advertising, advertising tracking, or third-party usage-analytics SDKs. It does not sell personal information.

7. Security

The current Android build does not enable DDS Security. DDS traffic and HTTP diagnostic requests are not encrypted by the app. Use it only on networks and systems you are authorized to access, and avoid transmitting personal or confidential information over unprotected connections.

8. External websites and distribution

If you open an external link, the destination website processes your visit under its own privacy policy. Google Play also processes information relating to app distribution under Google's policies.

This privacy-policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with requests to this website, such as your IP address. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement .

9. Contact and privacy requests

For questions or requests concerning personal information handled by the developer, contact trittsv.apps@gmx.de. The developer cannot access or delete information held solely on your device or by independent DDS network operators.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data-handling practices change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
